Connect your domain

Replace these examples with your domain, mail host, and server IP. Internet email arrives on port 25; forward it to the configured SMTP port. Allow inbound traffic through your firewall.

inbox.yourdomain.com.  MX  10 mail.yourdomain.com.
mail.yourdomain.com.   A      YOUR_SERVER_IP

The .localhost domains are for local testing. DNS changes are made with your DNS provider; adding a name above does not change DNS. Configure a TLS certificate on the receiver or place it behind a mail gateway for public use.
