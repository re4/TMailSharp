A LITTLE PEACE OF MIND

Your inbox, briefly.

A private browser cookie gives you access to the addresses you create. Clearing that cookie or switching browsers means losing access.

Messages and attachments are stored on this server until your inbox expires. Expired mail is immediately inaccessible and then removed by the database’s cleanup process. The server operator can access stored data and is responsible for its infrastructure, backups, and logs.

Scripts and remote images in emails are blocked. Attachments are downloaded as files, not opened in the site. Sender names and message contents are unverified.

No analytics or advertising trackers are included. Temporary mail is convenient, but it is not encrypted end-to-end. Keep passwords, banking, and sensitive personal information in your regular email.
